## Aircraft

Embraer Phenom 300 / EMB-505, S/N 50500096, PW535E-powered, registration history D-CHIC Germany to N301XT U.S. in 2025. Extracted airframe-side records span 2008-05-06 through 2029-06-07. Latest known recorded airframe utilization is TAT 9,330.4 hours / TAC 6,451 cycles on 2026-04-28 (N301XT scans p.18).

## Damage history

### Confirmed major damage events

**2014 Zadar / LDZA hail event — most consequential airframe event.** Records report hailstorm damage for D-CHIC in Croatia/Zadar on 2014-06-12 at TAT 897.17, with entries also describing hail and lightning damage in Zadar (D CHIC - L-AKTE - 1-11 p.204) (D CHIC - L-AKTE - 1-11 p.206). Damage was reported during approach to LDZA and involved LH/RH wing leading edges, radome, engine inlet/lip skin, and paint (D CHIC - L-AKTE - 1-11 p.201) (D CHIC - L-AKTE - 1-11 p.217) (D-CHIC - Mar 2014 to Aug 2014 p.126). Embraer issued temporary allowable damage and inspection/repair instructions; the radome was required to be replaced before next flight (D-CHIC - Mar 2014 to Aug 2014 p.134) (D-CHIC - Mar 2014 to Aug 2014 p.135). Nayak performed inspections at CGN/Cologne; HFEC inspections after hail damage found no crack indications, and the nose radome was replaced due to the hailstorm (D-CHIC - Mar 2014 to Aug 2014 p.116) (D-CHIC - Mar 2014 to Aug 2014 p.150). Follow-up 50-cycle/recurrent inspections were recorded as satisfactory, and on 2014-09-09 Nayak replaced wing leading edges and related films/seals at TAT 1,122.0 / TAC 797 (D-CHIC - Mar 2014 to Aug 2014 p.85) (D-CHIC - Mar 2014 to Aug 2014 p.28) (D-CHIC - Aug 2014 to Feb 2015 p.644).

**2016 hail strike — second major hail damage package.** On 2016-10-11, Nayak opened inspection/investigation at CGN at TAT 3,083.0 / TAC 2,151; records found dents and damaged protective films after hailstrike (D-CHIC - Oct 2016 to Mar 2017 p.813). Embraer documentation recorded dents on LH/RH wing leading edges, vertical stabilizer fairings, and radome damage, with ETD-2016-P300-02315827 providing temporary allowable damage, NDT, and repair guidance (D-CHIC - Oct 2016 to Mar 2017 p.621) (D-CHIC - Oct 2016 to Mar 2017 p.631) (D-CHIC - Oct 2016 to Mar 2017 p.771). Nayak installed speed tape and recorded wing leading-edge dents out of limits; eddy current NDT found no cracks (D-CHIC - Oct 2016 to Mar 2017 p.793) (D-CHIC - Oct 2016 to Mar 2017 p.813). Recurrent inspections through early 2017 were repeatedly recorded as satisfactory/no new damage, followed by final hail damage repair documentation by Atlas/CAMP in April–May 2017 (D-CHIC - Oct 2016 to Mar 2017 p.747) (D-CHIC - Oct 2016 to Mar 2017 p.129) (D-CHIC - May 2017 p.11) (D-CHIC - May 2017 p.225).

**2018 AMS lightning strike — major event with final inspections.** Records identify a lightning strike during approach to AMS on 2018-09-21 (D-CHIC - October 2018-July 2019 p.213). Atlas Air Service recorded lightning strike rectification and unscheduled checks in September 2018 (D-CHIC - October 2018-July 2019 p.208) (D CHIC - L-AKTE - 1-11 p.149). CAMP recorded lightning strike reinspection requirements in October 2018 (D-CHIC - October 2018-July 2019 p.544). Final lightning strike inspections were later recorded by Altenrhein/Altenrhein Aviation; one record states the final lightning strike inspection found no damage at TAT 5,160.56 / TAC 3,632 (D-CHIC - October 2018-July 2019 p.249).

**2021 ventral rudder lightning damage.** Embraer Aircraft Maintenance Services in Nashville recorded lightning strike damage on the ventral rudder assembly on 2021-05-19 (D-CHIC - Apr 2023 - Nov 2023 p.293). Embraer disposition two days later characterized the ventral rudder lightning damage as minor (D-CHIC - Apr 2023 - Nov 2023 p.283) (D-CHIC - Apr 2023 - Nov 2023 p.284).

**Recurring corrosion / delamination findings.** Records show repeated airframe corrosion findings after the hail events: RH inboard flap root-rib corrosion in 2017 (D CHIC - L-AKTE - 1-11 p.142); rudder attachment bracket, rudder boost spring, and LH elevator bearing/support corrosion in 2019, including replacement of the LH elevator bearing assembly (D-CHIC - July 2019 to Dec 2019 p.20) (D-CHIC - July 2019 to Dec 2019 p.100) (D-CHIC - July 2019 to Dec 2019 p.157). In 2022, Air Hamburg Technik repaired corrosion/delamination on all four flaps, treated center fuselage skin splice corrosion, treated corrosion around HF/ELT antenna areas, removed/reinstalled a rudder middle hinge fitting with corrosion/damage, replaced a corroded LH MLG uplock box, and repaired main door cutout corrosion (D-CHIC - Oct 2022 - Mar 2022 p.23) (D-CHIC - Oct 2022 - Mar 2022 p.24) (D-CHIC - Oct 2022 - Mar 2022 p.27) (D-CHIC - Oct 2022 - Mar 2022 p.30) (D-CHIC - Oct 2022 - Mar 2022 p.29).

### Minor / routine items

Minor items include repeated paint touch-ups, ELT visual inspections, antenna connector cleaning, access-door/nutplate corrosion replacement, and interior/stowage-box repairs; these are maintenance findings, not stand-alone major damage events (D-CHIC - Jan 2013 to Feb 2014 p.460) (D-CHIC - Mar 2015 to Oct 2015 p.101) (D-CHIC - Oct 2022 - Mar 2022 p.24).

## Maintenance & inspection program

The records show extensive European maintenance continuity, principally Nayak, Air Hamburg/Air Hamburg Technik, Atlas Air Service, Altenrhein Aviation, Embraer, and CAMP Systems. Nayak completed a 600FH/12-month inspection and annual maintenance check in January 2014 (D-CHIC - Jan 2013 to Feb 2014 p.79). Air Hamburg applied for an Airworthiness Review Certificate in January 2014 (D-CHIC - Jan 2013 to Feb 2014 p.18). The aircraft then received damage-driven inspections, Embraer dispositions, recurrent checks, and structural repair work tied to the 2014 and 2016 hail packages. Routine paint/interior work appears throughout the early records, including exterior paint repairs and passenger armrest work in 2013 (D-CHIC - Jan 2013 to Feb 2014 p.440) (D-CHIC - Jan 2013 to Feb 2014 p.454).

## Engine damage & notable findings

**CONFIRMED major damage / deterioration history**

- **Engine air inlet cracking was an early recurring issue.** Embraer SB 505-71-0006 mandated repetitive engine air inlet crack inspections in 2013 (D-CHIC - Mar 2014 to Aug 2014 p.249). Cracks were found during the 2013-11-25 SB inspection, including cracks in both engine air intakes (D-CHIC - Jan 2013 to Feb 2014 p.193). Earlier, the LH engine air inlet assembly was replaced after a crack was found (D-CHIC - Jan 2013 to Feb 2014 p.203). A later repetitive inlet inspection found a crack and broken rivet heads (D-CHIC - Aug 2014 to Feb 2015 p.852).
- **Hail-related inlet damage is recorded.** A 2014 entry reports hail damage to the L/H engine air intake (D CHIC - L-AKTE - 1-11 p.236).
- **Both inlet abradable liners had cracks/spots repaired.** Atlas Air Service inspected and repaired cracks/spots on both engine inlet abradable liners; the digest entry is undated (D-CHIC - Oct 2016 to Mar 2017 p.505).
- **Lightning-strike follow-up included engine chip detector and oil filter reinspection.** Atlas Air Service performed a lightning strike reinspection of engine chip detectors and oil filters in 2018 (D-CHIC - October 2018-July 2019 p.542).
- **2019 leak/corrosion findings need ESN/position reconciliation.** One PCE-DG0189 record describes “Engine 2” leak, crack, and corrosion findings with repairs and inspections at ETT 8,242.07 / ECYC 5,721 (Engine Log - PCE-DG0189 p.195). A DG0188 2 record at the same aircraft and engine times describes an LH engine leak and corrosion inspection, with O-rings and LVDT replaced (Engine log DG0188 2 p.2). The buyer should reconcile whether this was the same event recorded under conflicting position labels, or separate findings.
- **Exhaust corrosion became a recurring two-engine theme.** Corrosion on both exhaust nozzles was recorded in 2020 (D CHIC - L-AKTE - 1-11 p.142) and again in 2021 (D CHIC - L-AKTE - 1-11 p.142). ENG #2 exhaust was found corroded with missing/worn hardware (D-CHIC - Oct 2021 to July 2022 p.387). Engine 1 also had corroded exhaust hardware and exhaust stack corrosion (D-CHIC - Oct 2021 to July 2022 p.394). Subsequent entries record exhaust removal, corroded bolts, missing washers, worn nuts, and repair/signoff activity for both engines (D-CHIC - Oct 2021 to July 2022 p.388; D-CHIC - Oct 2021 to July 2022 p.395; D-CHIC - Oct 2021 to July 2022 p.394; D-CHIC - Oct 2021 to July 2022 p.310).
- **Bypass duct attachment corrosion was treated on both engines.** AIR HAMBURG Technik cleaned and treated corrosion on ENG 1 and ENG 2 bypass duct attachments in 2022 (D-CHIC - Oct 2022 - Mar 2022 p.24).
- **Accessory/component condition findings include starter-generator overheating and compressor-drive-module corrosion.** A 2019 starter-generator teardown found overheating and multiple worn/damaged parts (D-CHIC - July 2019 to Dec 2019 p.532). A 2023 overhauled compressor drive module was returned INOP with corrosion and a bent base plate (D-CHIC - Apr 2023 - Nov 2023 p.134).
